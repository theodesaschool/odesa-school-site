About Us

The Odesa School presents a privately held collection of paintings from the Odesa school, active between the 1930s and 1980s. Every work in the collection has been personally selected, researched, and documented by its owner.

Each painting is accompanied by supporting documentation — certificates of authenticity, provenance records, and, where available, the artist's own signature and archival material — so that collectors can acquire with confidence.

This site mirrors our Instagram, where new acquisitions and details from the collection are shared first: @theodesaschool.
