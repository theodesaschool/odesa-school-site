How to Buy

Each acquisition is handled personally, from first enquiry to delivery. We provide full details on the work, a delivery quote for your country and an invoice in your preferred currency.

    Delivery

    Paintings are shipped from Ukraine worldwide. The delivery cost and timing for your country are confirmed before payment.

    Payment

    We accept payment from the US, the UK, Europe, Ukraine and worldwide. Prices are listed in US dollars; invoices can be issued in US dollars, pounds, euros or hryvnia.

    Bank transfer

    US, UK, European and Ukrainian accounts, including Ukrainian bank cards.

    Revolut

    Fast transfers between Revolut accounts.

    Wise

    Competitive rates for cross-currency payments.

    PayPal

    Widely used and convenient internationally.

    • Payment details are provided exclusively in our invoice.
    • We do not request card details or passwords at any stage.
    • Paintings are dispatched once payment is received.
