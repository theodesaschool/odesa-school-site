Oleksandr Freidin

1926–1984 Painter of the Odesa "severe style"

Oleksandr Freidin (1926–1984) was an Odesa painter of genre scenes, landscapes and portraits. He was born in Odesa on 29 September 1926. His father, Borys Freidin, a party official, was arrested in January 1938 and shot later that year. During the war Freidin was evacuated with his mother to Buguruslan, where he worked as a fitter and diesel mechanic; they returned to Odesa in 1948.

From 1948 to 1951 he studied at the Odesa Art School, where his teachers included Liubov Tokareva-Aleksandrovych and Dina Frumina; he left in 1951 without completing the course. He then worked as a painter at the Odesa art-production combine and spent several years in the studio of Oleksandr Atsmanchuk, who painted his portrait in 1962. He exhibited from 1956 and joined the Union of Artists in 1964. In 1962 he showed the landscape Shore of Arcadia at the republican exhibition of marine painting.

He spent five years (1969–1974) on his main painting, You Are With Us, You Are With Us, Though You Are Not in the Columns, also known as Funeral in Madrid. According to the journalist Yevhen Holubovskyi, it was conceived as a requiem for the victims of Stalin's repressions, his father among them. Accused of formalism, the work was not admitted to the republican exhibition in Kyiv. The large canvas is now in a private collection in the USA, and the Museum of Odesa Modern Art holds a study for it. After 1974 he exhibited less and turned to the series Windows and Man and Bird and to portraits.

Freidin died in Odesa on 19 December 1984. The Odesa Art Museum held exhibitions of his work in 1986, 1996 and 2001. His paintings are in the Odesa National Art Museum, the Museum of Odesa Modern Art and the Norton and Nancy Dodge Collection at the Zimmerli Art Museum (USA). Holubovskyi placed him alongside Oleksandr Atsmanchuk, Yuriy Yegorov and Lev Mezhberg as one of the forerunners of contemporary art in Odesa.
