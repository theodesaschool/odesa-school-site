Mykola Shelyuto

1906–1984 Honoured Figure of Arts of Ukraine

Mykola Shelyuto (1906–1984) was a Ukrainian landscape painter and Honoured Figure of Arts of Ukraine. He was born on 22 February 1906 in the village of Glukhovka (now Gomel region, Belarus) and died in Odesa on 6 February 1984.

From 1925 to 1931 he studied at the Odesa Art Institute under Pavlo Volokidin, Teofil Fraerman and Mykhailo Zhuk. He exhibited from 1936 and joined the Union of Artists in 1938. During the Second World War he took part in the defence of Odesa and returned to the city in 1944.

For many years he taught painting and drawing at the Odesa Art School. His pupils included Kostiantyn Lomykin, Oleksandr Atsmanchuk, Lev Mezhberg and Volodymyr Lytvynenko, who later married his daughter Tamara. All four are represented in this collection. His son Oleksandr catalogued nearly 2,000 of his paintings.

He painted Odesa and its coast, Crimea and the Ukrainian countryside. In the words of art historian Tetiana Basanets, for fifty years he remained "a faithful continuer of the traditions of the South Russian school of painting". Ukrainian museums in Kyiv, Lviv, Odesa and Alushta hold more than 80 of his works. The Odesa Art Museum marked his centenary with a retrospective in 2006.
