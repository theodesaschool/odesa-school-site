Viktor Zhurakovskyi

1928–2001

Viktor Zhurakovskyi (1928–2001) was an Odesa painter of landscapes and seascapes. He was born on 29 May 1928 in Harbin, China, and died in Odesa on 19 September 2001.

He came to painting relatively late: at twenty-five he was working as a loader and a driver. From 1952 to 1955 he studied at the Odesa Art School, where his teachers included Dina Frumina and Mykhailo Todorov; the art historian Olha Tarasenko also names Oleksandr Atsmanchuk among those who shaped his early painting. Lev Mezhberg graduated in the same class. Zhurakovskyi exhibited from the mid-1950s and joined the Union of Artists in 1958.

He worked mainly in the open air, and water, above all the sea, appears in almost all of his paintings. Tarasenko describes his palette as a restrained "silvery-golden, mother-of-pearl" range. He had a solo exhibition in 1993. His works were acquired by museums in Odesa, Donetsk and Mykolaiv; the Kherson Art Museum holds his "Partisan Khadzhibey" (1977).
