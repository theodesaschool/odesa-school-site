Heorhiy Pavliuk

1925–1987 Honoured Artist of the Ukrainian SSR

Heorhiy Pavliuk (1925–1987) was an Odesa painter and an Honoured Artist of the Ukrainian SSR (1973). He was born in Odesa on 20 November 1925 and died there on 14 November 1987. He was the son of the painter Mykola Pavliuk, who taught Albin Gavdzinskiy and Volodymyr Lytvynenko, both represented in this collection. His wife, Yevheniia Pavliuk-Sudina, and his daughter, Halyna Pavliuk, are also artists.

In 1953 he graduated from the Kyiv Art Institute, where he studied under Serhiy Hryhoriev. Back in Odesa, he taught at the art school (1953–1966, with breaks) and at the Engineering-Construction Institute (1972–1975), and worked at the art-production combine. He joined the Union of Artists in 1953.

He worked mainly on thematic paintings with historical and contemporary subjects, such as Golden Wedding (1957), In the Blast-Furnace Shop (1960), Taras Shevchenko and Children (1969) and Sails of Our Youth (1981). He also painted portraits and landscapes. He exhibited from 1951 and had a solo exhibition in Odesa in 1968. His works are held by the National Art Museum of Ukraine, the Odesa Art Museum and art museums in Ivano-Frankivsk, Sumy, Khmelnytskyi and Chernihiv.
