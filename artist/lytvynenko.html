Volodymyr Lytvynenko

1930–2011 Honoured Artist of Ukraine

Honoured Artist of Ukraine, a pupil of Shelyuto. Lytvynenko inherited the tradition of the Odesa school and made it his own.

Born in Hadiach on 3 June 1930; died in Odesa on 1 May 2011. Between 1948 and 1956 he studied at the Odesa Art School under Hryhorii Kryzhevskyi, Mykola Pavliuk and Mykola Shelyuto; Kryzhevskyi and Shelyuto are also represented in this collection.

In 2011, the Odesa Art Museum showed 118 of his works in a posthumous exhibition. His paintings are also held in museums in Zaporizhzhia, Russia, and Greece.
