Oleksandr Atsmanchuk

1923–1974

Oleksandr Atsmanchuk (1923–1974) was an Odesa painter of portraits, landscapes and thematic pictures, and a teacher. He was born in Odesa on 8 September 1923 and died there on 7 October 1974.

He graduated from the Odesa Art School in 1947, where he studied under Leonid Muchnyk and Mykola Shelyuto, and from the Repin Institute in Leningrad in 1953, in the studio of Mykhailo Avilov. He exhibited from 1947 and joined the Union of Artists in 1954. In the 1950s he taught at the Odesa Art School. After graduating, Oleksandr Freidin, also represented in this collection, worked and studied in his studio for several years; Atsmanchuk painted his portrait in 1962.

Art historian Tetiana Basanets connects his name with the romantic style of the 1960s generation. In the late 1960s he turned to the legacy of the Renaissance and experimented with portrait painting; he was known as a fine draughtsman. His works include "Order Given…" (1957), "Flight" (1960–1967) and the unfinished "The Drummer" (1970–1974), as well as a series of Odesa landscapes painted from 1947 into the 1960s.

He received no honorary titles during his lifetime, and his first solo exhibition was held only after his death. The Odesa Art Museum marked his 90th anniversary with an exhibition in 2013. His works are held by the Odesa Art Museum, the National Art Museum of Ukraine and the Museum of the Russian Academy of Arts in St Petersburg.
