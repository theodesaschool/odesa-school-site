Albin Gavdzinskiy

1923–2014 People's Artist of Ukraine

Albin Gavdzinskiy (1923–2014) was an Odesa painter of landscapes, genre scenes and portraits, and a People's Artist of Ukraine. He was born on 4 July 1923 in the village of Bobryk Pershyi, Odesa region; his family moved to Odesa when he was a child. He died in Odesa on 12 May 2014.

He entered the Odesa Art School in 1940. During the war he trained as a pilot and served as a flight instructor at an Air Force school. He returned to painting in 1946 and graduated with honours in 1950; his teacher was Mykola Pavliuk.

From 1951 to 1960 he painted the construction of the Kakhovka hydroelectric power station, calling himself its "artistic chronicler". He gave the 237 paintings of this series to the town of Nova Kakhovka, where they became the basis of the town's picture gallery. He was made an honorary citizen of Nova Kakhovka in 1961, and in 2003 the gallery was named after him. In 2022, during the Russian occupation of the town, the collection was taken away; its whereabouts are unknown.

He was named Honoured Artist of Ukraine in 1976 and People's Artist of Ukraine in 2004, and for twenty years he sat on the board of the Odesa branch of the Union of Artists. His daughter Olena Gavdzinska is also a painter. In 2013 the Odesa Museum of Western and Eastern Art showed 162 of his paintings.
